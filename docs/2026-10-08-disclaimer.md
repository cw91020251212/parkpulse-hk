# 2026-10-08 — Bilingual disclaimer

Added a footer **使用聲明 / Disclaimer** entry that opens an accessible, mobile-readable dialog. It covers the reference-only nature of official and related-agency data, no availability or reservation guarantees, third-party Google Maps and Lands Department content, browser-only location use, and local browser preferences. The dialog is available in Traditional Chinese and English, receives focus, closes with its visible close button, backdrop click or Escape, and is documented in `docs/disclaimer.md` and the repository README.

The wording is a general product-use notice and explicitly says it is not legal advice.
